Tips
  • Install as a web app: Share → Add to Home Screen.
  • Tap a line to select it.
  • Drag an endpoint to rotate/resize.
  • Drag the line body to move it.
  • Controls hide after a moment; tap to show them again.
  • Swipe up from the bottom handle to show controls; they auto-collapse after 6 seconds.
  • Set R / L to match your handedness — this ensures the yellow swing-plane line angles correctly.
  • In S (side) view: the yellow line is your swing plane — auto-set at address. The club badge shows the detected club based on your arm angle. Use the target ⊙ button to re-snap.
