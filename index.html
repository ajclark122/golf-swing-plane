Tips
  • Install as a web app: Share → Add to Home Screen.
  • Tap a line to select it.
  • Drag an endpoint to rotate/resize.
  • Drag the line body to move it.
  • Controls hide after a moment; tap to show them again.
  • In Side view: the yellow line is your swing plane — auto-set at address, drag to adjust. Tap it then use the target button to re-calibrate.
